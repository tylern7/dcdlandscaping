Our services

We turn ideas into works of art.

Time commitment, care and cost effectiveness are our true strengths, and we let our work speak for itself across Pickering, Ajax, Whitby, Oshawa, Bowmanville and Courtice.

01

Driveways & extensions

An inviting driveway that leads you and your guests to the front door. Tired of shuffling cars in your pyjamas every winter morning? We will add that extra legal parking space.

02

Backyard patios

Small yard or large, we create a cozy patio where you can make memories and enjoy quality time with family and friends.

03

Steps & landings

Professional, safe stairs and landings to step up to your front door or down into the backyard.

04

Pool patios & coping

A functional, relaxing patio around your pool for dining and entertaining, safe for the kids to play. Our coping makes it easy to get in and out, or just sit on the edge and dip your feet.

05

Retaining walls & garden beds

Whether it holds back soil, carries structural load or frames a flower bed or vegetable garden, we build walls that complement your yard, front or back.

06

Waterfalls & water features

The tranquil, soothing sound of falling water to unwind with after a busy, stressful day at work.

Our story

Two shovels, a wheelbarrow and a lot of heart.

DCD Landscaping began as a humble husband and wife venture, driven by a love for turning ordinary yards into extraordinary retreats. Today the family-owned business has grown to include a talented team that shares that passion.

We provide environmentally conscious design and customized services for your outdoor spaces, transforming Durham yards into inviting places where families gather and create memories.
